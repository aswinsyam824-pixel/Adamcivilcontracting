01Overview

Keep your project moving forward

Our project management services help coordinate people, activities and information across construction and building-services work.

We work with the client and project stakeholders to establish clear priorities, track progress and address issues as they arise. The level of support is agreed to suit the project scope and delivery requirements.

03How we coordinate

Visibility, coordination and follow-through

  • Set the baseline

    Confirm scope, milestones, reporting needs and communication channels.

  • Coordinate workstreams

    Align design, procurement, site activities and building-services interfaces.

  • Track decisions and actions

    Maintain clear records of decisions, responsibilities and follow-up dates.

  • Report progress

    Provide updates on progress, upcoming activities and items requiring attention.

05Questions

Frequently asked questions

What projects can you support?

We can discuss project coordination for construction, fitout and building-services work, subject to project scope and resourcing.

Can you coordinate multiple contractors?

Coordination requirements can be agreed with the client and project stakeholders, including communication, activities and interfaces.

Do you provide progress reports?

Reporting frequency and format are agreed for the project and may include progress, upcoming activities, decisions and open actions.

How can I request project support?

Share the project location, scope, stage and required support through our enquiry form so we can review your needs.

Planning a project?

Tell us about your project scope, location and delivery stage. We will review the requirements and discuss next steps.
