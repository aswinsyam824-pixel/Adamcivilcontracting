01Overview

Complete fitout for every type of space

Whether it is an office, a shop, a restaurant or a home, we deliver complete fitouts that combine interior works with the civil and MEP services required for the space.

From the first layout to final inspection, our coordinated team manages design coordination, approvals, construction and handover. Approval requirements depend on the facility, location and agreed scope.

03Scope of works

What a fitout includes

  • Space planning and design coordination

    Layouts developed with you, your consultants and the landlord or building management.

  • Partitions, ceilings and flooring

    Gypsum and glass partitions, suspended ceilings and flooring to suit each space.

  • Joinery and built-in furniture

    Reception counters, cabinetry, wardrobes, partitions and custom joinery.

  • Painting, cladding and finishes

    Paint, wall cladding, feature walls and final finishing.

  • HVAC and ventilation

    Cooling, fresh air and extraction adapted to the new layout.

  • Electrical, lighting and data

    Power, lighting design, data points and supplementary supplies.

  • Plumbing and drainage

    Washrooms, pantries, kitchens and water supply.

  • Fire alarm and firefighting

    Fire detection, alarm and firefighting systems coordinated with the fitout.

04Authority approvals

All authority approvals, managed for you

Most fitouts need several approvals before work can start and before the space can open. We coordinate the process alongside the agreed scope and location requirements.

  1. 01

    Drawings and submissions

    Prepare and coordinate the fitout drawings and documents.

  2. 02

    Landlord approval

    Meet the landlord or building management submission requirements.

  3. 03

    Municipality permits

    Coordinate permits required for the project scope.

  4. 04

    Civil defence approval

    Coordinate fire and life-safety requirements for the fitout.

  5. 05

    Inspections and completion

    Support inspections, final approvals and completion before opening.

07Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a fitout take?

Timing depends on the space, scope, approvals and material lead times. After reviewing your requirements, we can outline a project programme.

Do you handle the authority approvals?

We coordinate the submission documents and follow-up for the agreed scope. Requirements and decisions are set by the relevant authorities.

Can you work while the premises are in use?

Where the scope allows, work can be phased and coordinated with the building team to help limit disruption.

Do you provide maintenance after handover?

Yes. Annual maintenance contracts can be discussed for the building systems included in your project.

Planning a fitout?

Share your location, floor area and the type of space. We will review your scope and come back with next steps.
