01Overview

Fitout specialised in healthcare

We coordinate healthcare fitouts from brief and space planning through construction and handover. Civil, interior and building-services work is brought together around the needs of your facility.

Our team can support the preparation and coordination of authority submissions. Approval requirements depend on the facility, location and scope, and are confirmed with the relevant authorities.

03Authority approvals

All authority approvals, managed for you

Healthcare facilities may require approvals from several authorities before they can open. We coordinate submission documents and follow-up for approvals relevant to the agreed scope.

  1. 01

    Drawings and submissions

    Prepare and coordinate the fitout drawings and documents.

  2. 02

    Health authority review

    Coordinate the health authority review for the facility.

  3. 03

    Municipality permits

    Coordinate permits required for the building and fitout.

  4. 04

    Civil defence approval

    Coordinate fire and life-safety requirements for the fitout.

  5. 05

    Inspections and completion

    Support inspections, final approvals and completion before opening.

06Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a healthcare fitout take?

Timing depends on the facility, scope, approvals and material lead times. After reviewing your requirements, we can outline a project programme.

Do you handle the authority approvals?

We coordinate the required submission documents and follow-up for the agreed scope. Requirements and decisions are set by the relevant authorities.

Can you work in a facility that is already operating?

Where the scope allows, works can be planned in phases and coordinated with the facility team to help limit disruption.

Do you provide maintenance after handover?

Yes. Annual maintenance contracts can be discussed for the building systems included in your project.

Planning a healthcare facility?

Share your location, floor area and the type of facility. We will review your scope and come back with next steps.
