01

Building contracting

Construction of buildings from structure through to finishing, coordinated with engineering consultants and handed over against the agreed scope.

Typical applications

  • New buildings
  • Extensions
  • Structural works
  • Finishing
02

Road contracting

Construction and repair of roads and paved areas, including sub-base and surface preparation and associated works such as kerbs and drainage.

Typical applications

  • Access roads
  • Parking areas
  • Internal site roads
  • Repairs
03

Service lines coring and ditching

Core drilling, concrete cutting and trenching to create routes for pipes, ducts and cables, planned around existing services.

Typical applications

  • Utility routes
  • Duct crossings
  • Wall and slab openings
04

Wrecking and demolition

Planned demolition and site clearance with safety controls, sequencing and removal of debris to prepare sites for new work.

Typical applications

  • Strip-out
  • Structure removal
  • Site clearance
