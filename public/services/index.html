Civil Construction and Infrastructure

  • Road contracting

  • Wrecking and demolition

Mechanical, Electrical and Cooling Systems

Air conditioning, ventilation and electromechanical installations, with power distribution and control equipment.

  • Air conditioning
  • Ventilation and air filtration
  • Electromechanical equipment
  • Electricity transmission and control equipment

Fire Safety, Gas and Specialized Piping

Supply, installation and maintenance of fire protection equipment, central gas distribution and specialised piping.

  • Firefighting safety equipment
  • Central gas distribution
  • Specialized piping and fittings

Equipment and Structural Works

Machinery installation, satellite systems, scaffolding and structural metal erection for active sites.

  • Equipment installation
  • Satellite receiving equipment
  • Scaffolding
  • Metal parts and erection

Landscaping and Pools

Soft and hard landscaping, irrigation and planting, plus routine swimming pool maintenance.

  • Landscape and gardening works
  • Swimming pool maintenance
06

Cleaning and Insulation

Tank and container cleaning, building cleaning and insulation works for new and existing properties.

  • Tanks and container cleaning
  • Building cleaning
  • Insulation contracting
07

Facilities Management Services

Coordinated facilities support to help keep buildings, assets and essential systems operating effectively.

  • Planned maintenance coordination
  • Building systems support
  • Facility service coordination

Project Management Services

Project coordination from planning through delivery, aligned with the agreed scope, programme and stakeholders.

  • Project planning and coordination

  • Stakeholder communication
