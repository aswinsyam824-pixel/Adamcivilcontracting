01About the project

The client needed a modern open-plan workspace with meeting rooms, a reception area and a pantry, delivered with minimal disruption to the building's other tenants.

Our team handled space planning coordination, authority approvals, interior works and the MEP services behind them, handing over a ready-to-use office.

Scope of works

  • Space planning coordination
  • Gypsum and glass partitions
  • Suspended ceilings
  • Joinery and reception counter
  • HVAC and electrical works
  • Authority approvals
