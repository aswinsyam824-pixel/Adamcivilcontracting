AboutAdam

A UAE contracting company bringing construction, installation and maintenance together under one coordinated team.

Contact our team

01Company overview

One contractor for the structure, the systems and their upkeep.

Adam Civil Contracting LLC works with owners, developers and procurement teams across the UAE on building and road works, mechanical, electrical and cooling systems, fire safety and specialised piping, and the cleaning, insulation and landscaping that keep properties performing.

Organising these services into six groups lets us review a scope as a whole, plan the trades together and give you a single point of contact from first enquiry to handover.

02Mission and vision

What we work towards
on every project

Our mission

To deliver dependable construction, installation and maintenance services through coordinated teams, clear communication and careful workmanship.

Our vision

To be a contracting partner that clients choose again, for the quality of the work and the way it is delivered.

04Delivery approach

How a project
moves with us

  1. Enquiry

    You share the service, location and scope by phone or email.

  2. Scope review

    We review drawings, site conditions and requirements with you.

  3. Proposal

    A clear proposal covering scope, method and programme.

  4. Execution

    A coordinated team delivers the works to the agreed standard.

  5. Handover

    Final checks, then the finished work is handed over.
