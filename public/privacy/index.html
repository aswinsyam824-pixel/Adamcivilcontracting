PrivacyPolicy

How Adam Civil Contracting handles information when you use this website or contact us. Effective 30 October 2026.

01

Who we are

This website is operated by Adam Civil Contracting LLC, United Arab Emirates. For any privacy question, contact us at info@adamcivilcontracting.ae or +971 4 355 7666.

02

Information collected when you visit

This website has no user accounts, contact forms, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not set cookies. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically records standard technical information when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time. This is used to deliver the website securely and reliably.

03

Information you send us

If you call or email us, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, company, contact details and project information. We use them only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations and manage any resulting work. We do not sell your information.

04

Third-party services

To display the website, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and a smooth-scrolling script from the jsDelivr content network. These providers receive your IP address and technical request details when those files load, under their own privacy policies. Links to other websites are governed by those websites’ policies.

05

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry correspondence only as long as needed to respond to you and for our normal business and legal records.

06

Your choices and questions

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing info@adamcivilcontracting.ae. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible.
